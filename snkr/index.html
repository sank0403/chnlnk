HOW TO PLAY?


The objective of CHN LNK is to identify all the words in the CHAIN - One letter at a time. Every word on the chain LINKS logically, with the word below it.
  • VOWELS (Yellow on grid) are Disabled till you figure out all the OTHER LETTERS in the chain.

  • CLICK to guess a consonant. If the letter you selected is in ANY WORD of the chain, the corresponding tiles will be highlighted in Green.

  • EVERY WORD LINKS WITH THE NEXT to form a common phrase.
    FIRE
    TRUCK
    STOP
    SIGN
    POST
  • Each incorrect letter costs you 1 Life. You begin with 5 lives, shown as ⚪ on the game board.
  • If you complete the entire chain, your remaining lives convert into ⭐'s. You can earn up to 5 ⭐'s each day.
  • Play 4 Consecutive Days to activate the Saftey Net.
  • Once Active — 1 Incorrect Entry won’t cost a life!
  • Resets once used or if you miss playing a day.
  • Timed mode can be enabled only at the start of the game. A 1 Minute Timer is added for each try.
  • You lose a life if you don’t make a correct guess within the minute.
  • A Perfect Solve in Timed Mode awards 3 Dynamites.
  • Finishing the game in Timed Mode without using dynamites grants 1 Extra Star.
  • Every day, one Mystery Letter is chosen at random.
  • Identify it in the Next Try to earn bonus Dynamites.
    • Normal Mode → +1 Dynamite.
    • Timed Mode → +2 Dynamites.
  • Dynamite eliminates Three Random invalid keys.
  • Invalid keys are letters that:
    • Do not appear in any future words.
    • Are still active on the keyboard.
  • See Mystery Letter and Perfect Solve to learn how to earn Dynamites.
  • No mistakes — no wrong letters.
  • No dynamite — using one disqualifies a Perfect Solve.
  • Rewards:
    • Normal Mode → +2 Dynamites.
    • Timed Mode → +3 Dynamites.
  • When you reach your final life, you get a one‑time offer:
    • Trade 2 Saved Stars
    • For 1 Extra Life
  • This offer applies only Once per day.
  • Play every day and build your streak.
  • Every 10‑Day Streak earns 3 Dynamites.
  • Earn Badges at streaks of 10(🥉), 25(🥈), 50(🥇), and 100(💎).
  • Finish in the Top 3 of the Monthly Leaderboard to win bonus Dynamites.
