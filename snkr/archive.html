HOW TO PLAY?


The objective of CHN LNK is to identify all the words in the CHAIN - One letter at a time. Every word on the chain LINKS logically, with the word below it.
  • CLICK to guess a letter. If the letter you selected is in ANY WORD of the chain, the corresponding tiles will be highlighted in Green.

  • VOWELS (Red on grid) are Disabled till you figure out all the OTHER LETTERS in the chain.

  • See the TUTORIAL of solving a chain. Notice how vowels are disabled initially, and how each word meaningfully links with the next one : FOREST TRAIL → TRAIL MIX → MIX TAPE → TAPE MEASURE → MEASURE STEP → STEP LADDER.
